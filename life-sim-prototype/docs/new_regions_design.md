# New Regions — Visual & Clothing Design

Five new regions expanding outward from the Neighborhood. Each brings its own clothing style, architecture, and tone — but characters from the neighborhood should always look recognizable, even dressed differently.

---

## Region 1: THE OLDE TOWN — Medieval Gothic

### Vibe
A small walled town built centuries ago, now rediscovered as the Dream Realm barrier weakened. Stone and iron, candlelight, cobblestone. **Not gloomy** — the gothic is in the architecture (pointed arches, iron fixtures, gargoyle gutters, dark wood beams) not in the mood. People live here. Markets run. Kids play in the square. The darkness is in the *history*, not the current weather.

Think: a Dragon Quest town like Gondolia or L'Academie meets a touch of Yharnam's architecture (minus the horror).

### Architecture
- Stone buildings with dark timber frames and peaked slate roofs
- Narrow winding streets with iron lanterns and hanging signs
- A central square with a stone well and market stalls
- A small church/chapel with a pointed steeple and stained glass
- A smithy with a forge glow visible from the street
- Ivy and moss on old walls — the town is *old*, not abandoned
- Iron gates and stone walls at the town boundary

### The Backyard: Magical Rainforest & Jungle
Behind the town walls, pressing right up against them — a **magical rainforest** that shouldn't exist at this latitude. The Dream Realm energy that resurfaced the town also supercharged the surrounding landscape:
- Trees 3–5x normal size with glowing sap veins
- Bioluminescent flowers and mushrooms
- Vines that move slowly on their own
- Ancient ruins half-consumed by growth (stone arches, broken statues)
- Wildlife that's slightly wrong — too intelligent, too colorful, too large
- Deeper in the jungle = more Dream-warped and dangerous

This is the **Overgrowth** zone from the earlier design, connected directly to the town.

### Clothing — Medieval Adapted

> **Design rule:** These are modern neighborhood kids wearing old-world clothes *their way*. Not cosplay — they actually need this gear to survive here. But their personality shows through.

| Character | Medieval Outfit | How Their Modern Identity Shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Human Male (protagonist)** | Leather vest over loose linen shirt, rolled sleeves, fitted dark trousers, boots, belt with pouches, short sword at hip | He rolls the sleeves like he rolled his hoodie sleeves. Pouches where his pockets used to be. Still looks relaxed. |
| **Human Female** | Fitted bodice over flowing blouse, split riding skirt for mobility, knee boots, fingerless leather gloves, cloak over one shoulder | She wears the cloak like she'd wear a jacket — draped, not fastened. The split skirt says "I'm still going to fight in this." |
| **Wolf Beastfolk** | Fur-lined leather armor vest (no shirt — his fur IS the layer), heavy leather pants, armored boots, broadsword on back | The medieval look suits him *more* than modern clothes did. He was born for this era. |
| **Dragon-Hybrid Female** | Dark fitted tunic with back cutouts for wings, corset-style belt at waist, tight pants, heeled boots, silver rings on her horns | Gothic elegance. She doesn't look like she's visiting — she looks like she *owns* this aesthetic. |
| **Cat-Hybrid Female** | Light leather armor with a hooded cloak (ears poking through the hood), fitted pants, soft boots for stealth | The hood gives her cat ears a silhouette that looks like the hood's own shape — she can pass for human at a distance. |
| **Shark Aquatic Male** | Open chainmail vest, heavy belt, reinforced pants, no shoes (his feet are too broad), carries a massive anchor-like weapon | He looks terrifying. The medieval villagers are nervous around him. He doesn't notice. |

### Key Items
- **Swords** — from short swords to greatswords, fitting the tier system (Starter → Copper → Steel → Tideforged)
- **Staves/wands** — for the magic path
- **Cloaks and hoods** — functional, not decorative. Weather protection + identity concealment
- **Leather armor** — practical, not plate mail. These aren't knights.

## Region 2: THE RESURFACED CITY — Ancient Egypt

### Vibe
Millions of years ago, an entire Egyptian civilization was pulled into the Dream Realm when the barrier first formed. Now, as the barrier weakens, the city is **pushing back through the earth**. It didn't decay — it was preserved in the Dream Realm's timeless space. It surfaces *intact*, ancient but pristine, into the modern world.

The ground cracks open. Sand pours out. Obelisks rise from farmland. Pyramids emerge from hillsides. An entire landscape reshapes itself over days.

### Architecture & Landscape

**The Pyramids & Temples**
- Three massive pyramids in a line, the largest still partially underground (only the top third visible — the rest is explorable below)
- A temple complex with painted columns (the colors are vivid because the city was preserved, not weathered)
- Sphinx-like guardian statues — but they resemble the beastfolk god-guards, not traditional sphinxes
- Obelisks with hieroglyphic cartouches that glow faintly at night

**Sand Villages**
- Small settlements in the sand dunes surrounding the pyramids
- Mud-brick houses with flat roofs and cloth awnings
- Market bazaars with spice stalls, textile merchants, pottery sellers
- Irrigation channels from a small oasis (water is precious here)
- Villagers are a mix of humans and beastfolk who were trapped in the Dream Realm with the city and have been living there for millennia — to *them*, only a few generations have passed

**The Landscape**
- Desert sand dunes (golden, not monotone — salmon pink at dawn, deep amber at sunset)
- The oasis with palm trees, lotus pools, and reeds
- Rocky outcroppings with cave entrances leading to the underground city
- The "scar" where the city pushed through — a visible geological rupture at the region's edges, modern earth on one side, ancient sand on the other

### THE QUEEN — Nefertari (working name)

The ruler of the resurfaced city. She was queen when the city was pulled into the Dream Realm, and she has ruled it ever since — preserved by Dream energy, she has not aged. She has governed her people through millennia of timeless isolation. She is:

- **Dominant and bossy** — not evil, but absolutely accustomed to being obeyed. She has ruled longer than most civilizations have existed.
- **Beautiful and imposing** — dark skin, tall, regal posture. Elaborate golden headdress with cobra and vulture symbols. Dramatic kohl-lined Toriyama eyes (large but *sharp* — commanding, not soft). Golden collar necklace, arm bands, and belt. Fitted white linen dress.
- **Intelligent and strategic** — she immediately understands the modern world is different and begins adapting. She doesn't panic. She assesses.
- **A staff of authority** — not just symbolic. It channels the ancient power source.
- **Her relationship with the player:** She views the modern world as hers to reclaim. The player is an interesting curiosity — potentially useful, potentially an obstacle. She is neither ally nor enemy initially. She is a *queen dealing with a commoner*.

### THE GOD-GUARDS — Beastfolk Who Resemble Egyptian Gods

The Queen's personal guard are **beastfolk** whose animal types correspond to the Egyptian pantheon. They've served the royal line since the city was first built. They are:

| Guard | God Reference | Animal Type | Role | Personality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Anubis Guard** | Anubis (death/judgment) | Jackal-headed, tall, lean | Judge and executioner. Decides who enters the queen's presence. | Silent. Watches everything. Speaks only to deliver verdicts. |
| **Bastet Guard** | Bastet (protection/cats) | Cat-headed female, graceful | The queen's personal bodyguard. Always at her side. | Loyal, fierce, slightly playful when off-duty. Mid-spectrum beastfolk — more human-like than the others. |
| **Horus Guard** | Horus (sky/war) | Falcon-headed, winged | Military commander. Leads the city's defense forces. | Proud, strategic, respects strength. The one most likely to challenge the player to prove themselves. |
| **Sobek Guard** | Sobek (power/crocodiles) | Crocodile-headed, massive | The enforcer. The biggest, strongest guard. | Intimidating but surprisingly gentle off-duty. Loves food. Kids climb on him. |

All four wear **Egyptian ceremonial armor** — gold and bronze with turquoise/lapis accents, styled after traditional Egyptian military dress but fitted for their beastfolk bodies.

### Clothing — Egyptian

| Character | Egyptian Outfit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Player (visiting)** | Light linen wrap shirt, loose linen pants, sandals, a head wrap for sun protection | They look like a tourist trying their best. The queen finds it amusing. |
| **Egyptian Humans** | Traditional Egyptian dress — pleated white linen, broad collars, gold jewelry, kohl eyes | Pristine because the city was preserved. No weathering. |
| **Egyptian Beastfolk** | Same Egyptian dress modified for their bodies — the jackal-headed wear pharaonic kilts, the cat-headed wear fitted linen with gold arm bands | Their clothing is ceremonial and identifies their role/rank |
| **The Queen** | The most elaborate outfit in the game. White linen dress, golden headdress, collar, arm bands, belt, anklets. Her staff. | She should be the most visually impressive character the player has met. |

## Region 3: THE UNDERGROUND CITY — Beneath the Pyramids

### Vibe
Beneath the largest pyramid lies the true city — not tombs, but a **living underground metropolis** that was the civilization's real center. The surface structures were administrative and religious. The people *lived* below, where it was cool and protected.

Now, something else lives down here too — the **evil spirit** that was sealed when the city was first pulled into the Dream Realm. The barrier weakening freed the city *and* its prisoner.

### Environment
- Massive carved chambers lit by ancient glowing crystals embedded in the ceiling (artificial stars)
- Underground canals with still, dark water reflecting the crystal light
- Residential districts with stone houses carved from the bedrock
- A palace complex deeper down where the spirit has claimed territory
- Hieroglyphic murals that **tell the story** of why the city was sealed — a warning to anyone who reads them
- The deeper you go, the more the architecture warps — Dream energy corrupting the stonework, impossible geometry, rooms that are bigger inside than outside

This is the **Undercity** from the earlier design — the kid in the hoodie standing in front of the sealed door. That door leads *here*.

## Region 4: THE COSMIC PLANET

### Vibe
Accessible via a portal in the deepest part of the Underground City (or via the Dream Realm at its most extreme depth). This is not Earth. This is the origin point — where the forces that created the Dream Realm, the barrier, and the ancient power sources all came from.

Think: King Kai's planet meets Namek meets the Tournament of Power arena from Dragon Ball Super. Alien, beautiful, ancient, cosmic.

### Environment
- **Crystalline formations** growing from the ground like natural skyscrapers — deep purple, teal, amber
- **Two suns** — one large orange, one small blue — casting double shadows
- **Floating islands** connected by natural stone bridges and energy streams
- **Alien vegetation** — bioluminescent spiral trees, building-sized mushrooms, color-shifting flowers
- **A distant alien city** built into a crystal mountain — geometric architecture with organic curves
- **Ringed gas giant** visible in the sky, enormous and close
- **Thinner atmosphere** — stars visible during daytime
- The ground is dusty lavender/mauve with patches of teal grass

### Clothing — Cosmic
Characters don't change clothes for this region — they arrive in whatever they're wearing and look completely out of place. A kid in a hoodie on an alien planet. That contrast is the point. If anything, they might find alien accessories (energy-based jewelry, light-construct cloaks) but their core outfit stays Earth-based.

### Why This Region Exists
This is where the **cosmological connections** between all the games converge:
- The forces that created the Dream Realm originated here
- The Light/Darkness RPG's barrier was built using knowledge from here
- The ancient Egyptian civilization discovered a portal here — that's what drew the attention that got their city sealed
- The evil spirit beneath the pyramids is from here
- Grandpa's "ridiculous stories" were about a brief visit here

## Clothing Transition Summary — The Bible's Rule in Action

The visual bible says: *"The person comes first. Culture changes their clothing."*

Here's the same protagonist across all regions:

| Region | Outfit | What It Says |
|---|---|---|
| **Neighborhood** | Green hoodie, jeans, skate shoes | This is who I am |
| **Old Shore** | Same hoodie, board shorts, flip-flops | I'm visiting family |
| **Medieval Town** | Leather vest, linen shirt, boots, sword | I'm adapting to survive |
| **Egyptian City** | Linen wrap, loose pants, sandals | I'm a guest in someone else's world |
| **Underground** | Whatever they wore down + a torch and fear | I'm in over my head |
| **Cosmic Planet** | Hoodie and jeans on an alien world | I don't belong here. That's the point. |
