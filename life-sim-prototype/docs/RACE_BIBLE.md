# Race Bible

## Purpose

This document is the source of truth for the major peoples/races of the game. It defines racial identity, visual
language, body construction, cultural possibilities, transformations/awakenings, character-creator requirements,
clothing behavior, gameplay-facing visual rules, and what MUST remain consistent across forms.

Consult this BEFORE creating race-specific models, heads, bodies, clothing, hairstyles, transformations, NPCs,
animations, or character-creator options.

Correction documents describe failures. This document describes the target. See also `VISUAL_DIRECTION_BIBLE.md`.

## 1. Core philosophy

The world contains multiple intelligent peoples, but they must not feel like unrelated fantasy presets. Every race
exists inside the same world and the same visual language established by the main art-direction bible:

Modern / Rolling Tides city-skater life → medieval fantasy → gothic supernatural undertones → ancient civilizations /
Egypt → larger cosmic reality.

A race must therefore work in ALL of these environments. A character should be able to wear modern streetwear, skater
clothing, work clothes, athletic clothing, medieval clothing, fantasy armor, Ancient Egyptian-inspired regional
clothing and ceremonial clothing without suddenly looking like a character from another game.

**The person comes before the race.** Race affects anatomy, history and certain abilities. It does NOT completely
determine personality, morality, profession, intelligence, clothing style, social class, interests, hometown, religion
or fighting style.

A Beastfolk character can be a city skater. A dwarf can be a musician. An elf can dress emo. A human can be an ancient
warrior. A barbarian can be scholarly. **Culture and race are separate concepts.**

## 2. The awakening rule

Different peoples interact with supernatural power differently. Do NOT give every race the same glowing Super
Saiyan-style transformation with a different color.

Each awakening answers: *what is buried inside this people?* Their transformation reveals something about their origin.

| Race | Awakening reveals |
|---|---|
| Humans | development without an innate racial transformation |
| Ancient Cousins | ancestral biology resurfaces |
| Elves | celestial identity is revealed |
| Beastfolk | animal ancestry becomes dominant |
| Dwarves | body becomes an extension of craft, earth and endurance |
| Barbarians | spiritual bond with the natural world manifests physically |

Each must have a DIFFERENT silhouette and transformation language.

## 3. Humans

**Theme: ADAPT. CREATE. BELONG.**

Humans are intentionally the most visually ordinary race. This is not a weakness — humans provide the baseline against
which the stranger races become extraordinary. They have enormous cultural variety and exist throughout modern,
medieval and ancient societies.

### Appearance

Human anatomy establishes the standard humanoid body system. Support: masculine and feminine builds; lean, average,
athletic, stocky, strong; multiple heights where technically feasible; broad skin-tone range; diverse facial
structures; diverse hair textures; extensive clothing customization.

Modern humans should strongly preserve the Rolling Tides aesthetic — hoodies, oversized shirts, jeans, cargos, skate
shoes, high tops, jackets, alternative fashion, athletic wear, work clothes.

### Human progression

Humans DO NOT possess an automatic racial transformation. Do not compensate by giving humans a generic aura form.
Their strength is adaptability.

Progression should primarily come through training, equipment, magic, learned techniques, weapons, relationships,
artifacts, professions, player choices, and supernatural events specific to an individual.

A particular human can transform because of STORY circumstances. That does not mean humans as a species have a
transformation.

## 4. Ancient human cousins

**Working identity: LOST STARS. AWAKENED BLOOD.**

A distant evolutionary cousin of humanity. Humans and the Ancient Cousins descend from an extremely old common
ancestral population. At some distant point their evolutionary paths separated between worlds — humans developed along
one path, the Ancient Cousins evolved on another planet under very different conditions. They remain human enough that
the relationship is recognizable.

### Normal form

Should initially appear almost human. Subtle differences may include unusual eyes, slightly sharper canines, denser
musculature, biological markings that are normally nearly invisible, small anatomical differences, unusually high
physical potential. They should still be capable of blending into a human population.

### Primal Form

Ancient Cousins possess ONE major racial transformation: **Primal Form**. There are NOT Primal 1, 2, 3. Progression
comes from mastering the same transformation.

The inspiration is the IDEA of a primal ancestral transformation rather than copying any existing anime
transformation. The core fantasy: *something ancient that evolution buried inside the body becomes active again.* The
transformed character must remain recognizable.

### Hair rule

Primal Form preserves the player's selected hair TYPE. Do NOT replace every hairstyle with one transformation
hairstyle. Instead, the normal hairstyle becomes an evolved version of that hairstyle:

| Base | Primal |
|---|---|
| Curly | larger, longer, sharper curls |
| Locs | longer/wilder locs with greater movement |
| Straight | longer or dramatically swept structure |
| Braids | extended or loosened transformation variant |
| Afro | larger and more powerful silhouette |
| Short textured | more pronounced upward/outward structure |

The transformed hair should communicate: *this is still MY character.*

### Ancestral markings

Primal Form exposes biological ancestral markings. These are NOT tattoos — they are innate patterns normally hidden or
dormant within the body. Possible locations: face, neck, shoulders, chest, back, arms, legs.

Patterns should resemble organic branching energy rather than random glowing lines. Characters can possess different
marking patterns; these become part of racial customization.

## 5. Controlled Primal Form

The mastered expression: maximum ancestral power + conscious control. It uses a three-part energy identity.

- **Blue** — primary aura / supernatural energy; the dominant external aura.
- **Fiery orange** — the ancient biological power underneath the supernatural energy. Should appear like internal
  heat, embers or fire moving underneath/through the blue.
- **Blonde/gold lightning highlights** — the hair retains its original base identity but develops dramatic gold
  lightning-like highlights. These should NOT simply turn the character blonde. Imagine energetic streaks cutting
  through the player's existing hairstyle; the highlights follow the hairstyle. They may resemble lightning forks,
  glowing tips, illuminated strands or branching gold streaks.

The result should feel flashy and heroic without destroying character identity.

**Eyes:** unusually vivid — bright electric blue / blue-gold. Intensely alive rather than monstrous.

**Silhouette:** hair becomes slightly longer, larger, more angular, more energetic. Body becomes more defined,
slightly more muscular, visibly powerful — avoid extreme bodybuilder inflation. Clothing remains recognizable. The
player should still look like the character they created.

## 6. Uncontrolled / Dark Primal Form

Primal power can manifest incorrectly when driven by rage, terror, desperation, trauma, loss of control or
overwhelming survival instinct.

This is NOT a separate transformation tier. It is the same biological awakening without proper control.

- **Hair:** becomes WHITE, but the player's hairstyle is still preserved — longer, rougher, less organized, more
  aggressive, visually chaotic. It should look like the character's hairstyle pushed into an uncontrolled state.
- **Eyes:** glow RED. They should immediately communicate danger and diminished control.
- **Markings:** dark red, crimson, black-red, irregular, more aggressive. Instead of clean energy circulation, the
  patterns may appear overloaded.
- **Energy:** avoid copying the Controlled blue/orange aura. Dark Primal uses crimson, deep red, black and occasional
  white-hot energy. Energy should feel unstable.

**Story rule:** Dark Primal is NOT automatically evil. It represents power without balance. A good character can enter
Dark Primal. A villain can master Controlled Primal. Morality and transformation state are separate.

## 7. Elves

**Theme: TWO FORMS. ONE SOUL.**

Elves are celestial beings living among terrestrial peoples. The typical fantasy elf appearance is NOT their human
disguise — it is closer to their TRUE appearance.

### Human Veil

Elves can transform into a completely convincing human appearance. The Human Veil hides pointed ears, celestial
markings, unusual eyes, supernatural traits and other obviously elven characteristics. An elf can live among humans
without being immediately recognized.

### Celestial Form

The elf's natural/true state. Possible characteristics: pronounced pointed ears, elegant facial structure, slightly
elongated proportions, unusual luminous eyes, celestial markings, subtle star-like skin details, ethereal hair
effects, supernatural presence.

Color direction: violet, lavender, silver, moonlight blue, white, subtle gold. Avoid making their aura resemble Primal
energy — elf energy should feel quiet, cosmic and ancient, not explosive.

### Elf hair and clothing

Hair remains recognizable between Human Veil and Celestial Form. The Celestial version may become longer, more
flowing, slightly luminous, more elegant — but should preserve the original hairstyle's identity.

Elves can wear ordinary clothing. An elf should be allowed to look like a skater, an emo teenager, a musician, a shop
employee, an adventurer, a mage, a knight or an Egyptian-region resident. Celestial transformation does NOT
automatically replace their clothing with robes.

### Thematic connection

Elves can have an unusual relationship with memory, time, stars, souls and ancient history. Their long lives can
create strange relationships with memory — very old memories may gradually feel more like dreams.

## 7b. Decision 2026-09-18: Beastfolk splits into three families

The user decided to separate the animal peoples into three families for easier classification:

| Family | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Beastfolk** | mammals: canine, feline, vulpine, lagomorph, bear, avian and other furred/feathered lineages |
| **Aquatic folk** | shark, fish, octopus, eel, manta, coral and other sea lineages |
| **Draconic folk** | dragons, lizards, crocodilians and other scaled/horned lineages (horned, crested, winglet, smooth-scale) |

All three share ONE concept and ONE system. Each character sits somewhere on a spectrum:

```
human with traits  ------------------------------------------->  animal humanoid
(fins, horns, paws, scales or fur on an         (fully scaled/furred, animal head,
 otherwise human face and body)                  still upright and wearing normal clothes)
```

A **full beast form** (the real animal, e.g. the four-legged wolf in the beastfolk spectrum sheet) is linked to the
spectrum LATER as the transformation. It is not part of the everyday spectrum.

Sections 8, 9 and 16 below were written when all three were one "Beastfolk" race. Read "Beastfolk" there as applying
to all three families unless a section says otherwise. Aquatic and draconic examples in those sections
(lizard, crocodilian, aquatic, shark/fish) now belong to their own family.

## 8. Beastfolk

**Theme: MANY FORMS. ONE FAMILY.**

Beastfolk is ONE major racial classification containing humanoid peoples with animal ancestry. Do NOT split every
animal into an unrelated race.

Possible lineages: canine, feline, vulpine, rabbit/hare, bear, reptilian, lizard, crocodilian, avian, aquatic,
shark/fish, and additional lineages later. Lizardfolk fall underneath Beastfolk. Fish/aquatic humanoids also fall
underneath Beastfolk.

### Normal Beastfolk form

Should NOT normally look like completely anthropomorphic animals. The desired direction is **human + animal hybrid**,
retaining substantial humanoid facial readability.

Possible traits: humanlike facial structure, animal ears, tails, slightly altered nose, unusual eyes, subtle
fur/scales, claws/nails, fangs, horns/crests where appropriate, lineage-specific skin/fur markings.

Think: **person first, animal ancestry second.** This makes them compatible with the same emotional facial-animation
system.

| Lineage | Normal form |
|---|---|
| Feline | humanlike face, feline ears, tail, subtle feline eyes, small fangs |
| Canine | humanlike face, canine ears, tail, slightly stronger canine teeth |
| Lizard | humanlike skull proportions, scales, reptilian eyes, possible tail, claws, optional crest |
| Aquatic | humanlike face, aquatic ears/fins, subtle scales, unusual eyes, possible gill structures |

## 9. Beastfolk awakening

Awakening pushes them significantly closer to their animal ancestry. They remain intelligent and upright but become
substantially more animalistic.

Depending on lineage: facial structure becomes more animal-like; fangs become larger; claws extend; fur spreads;
scales become heavier; ears become more pronounced; tails become larger/stronger; pupils change; posture changes;
musculature changes; movement becomes more animalistic.

| Lineage | Awakened |
|---|---|
| Feline | stronger feline muzzle, larger claws, thicker fur, predatory eyes |
| Canine | wolf-like facial structure, heavier coat, larger teeth |
| Lizard | heavier scales, stronger jaw, claws, larger tail/crest |
| Aquatic | more pronounced fins/gills, predatory aquatic characteristics |

**Color:** do NOT assign Beastfolk one universal glowing aura. Their awakening is primarily BODY-BASED — the
transformation itself creates the spectacle. Small lineage-specific effects are acceptable (feline eye glow, reptilian
scale iridescence, aquatic bioluminescent markings, avian luminous feather accents), but these should support the
physical transformation rather than replace it.

## 10. Dwarves

**Theme: BUILD. INNOVATE. ENDURE.**

Preserve recognizable fantasy dwarf DNA while avoiding the stereotype that every dwarf is simply axe + beard + beer +
mine. Their defining trait is craftsmanship.

**Body:** shorter stature, broad torso, strong limbs, dense build. Body diversity still exists — not every dwarf is
muscular. Support lean craftspeople, average builds, stocky builds, heavily muscular builds, masculine/feminine
presentations.

**Culture:** engineering, architecture, smithing, mechanical invention, jewelry, underground construction, ancient
machinery, magical technology. Their societies can help bridge medieval fantasy and later technological development.

### Dwarven awakening — STONEHEART

Unlike Ancient Cousins, dwarves should NOT receive an aura-heavy biological transformation. Their awakening is BODY +
CRAFT: the dwarf becomes temporarily integrated with the materials/equipment they have mastered.

Possible effects: stone-like skin sections, metallic reinforcement, glowing runic seams, armor physically
synchronizing with the body, increased weight and stability, reinforced hands/forearms, crystalline or mineral
accents. This should make them resemble a living masterwork rather than a glowing human.

**Color:** primary steel, stone, bronze, amber; secondary cool rune blue and forge orange. Avoid using the Ancient
Cousins' blue/orange/gold combination in the same way. Glow should appear inside engraved runes, cracks, machinery and
equipment rather than surrounding the entire character.

## 11. Barbarians

**Classification:** barbarians are currently treated as a HUMAN CULTURAL BRANCH rather than a completely separate
biological species. However, their culture possesses a unique spiritual awakening tradition significant enough to
receive its own character-creation identity. "Barbarian" is therefore cultural rather than biological.

**Theme: WILD SPIRITS. STRONGER TOGETHER.**

Barbarian societies have retained an unusually strong relationship with nature, spirits, ancient rituals, survival,
elemental forces and ancestral traditions. They should NOT simply be unintelligent muscular warriors — barbarian
cultures contain warriors, hunters, healers, storytellers, spiritual leaders, musicians, craftspeople, farmers and
explorers.

**Visual language:** layered leather, woven fabric, fur where culturally/environmentally appropriate, wraps, tattoos,
braids, charms, bones/horns used carefully, handcrafted jewelry, heavy boots, practical survival gear. Different clans
should look adapted to their environment.

## 12. Bokai awakening

**Bokai** (working name) is a spiritual awakening rather than biological evolution. The body becomes an expression of
the person's connection to land + spirit + element + ancestry.

This awakening must have its OWN color direction. Do NOT reuse Ancient Cousin blue/orange/gold, elf violet/celestial
light, or Beastfolk animal transformation.

**Color direction:** EARTH + SPIRIT. Suggested universal underlying palette: deep teal, jade, turquoise, earthy brown,
bone white. Individual clans/elements then introduce additional colors.

| Clan | Palette |
|---|---|
| Forest Bokai | jade, moss, pale spirit light |
| Mountain Bokai | stone grey, turquoise, white |
| Desert Bokai | sand, turquoise, deep indigo |
| Frost Bokai | pale cyan, white, dark navy |
| Storm Bokai | deep indigo, pale electric cyan |
| Ember Bokai | charcoal, molten amber |

Ember does NOT need to become the default barbarian look. Bokai should generally be recognizable by its spiritual
turquoise/jade undertone, regardless of elemental variation.

**Body changes:** transformation can modify tattoos, hair, eyes, body markings, clothing, spiritual accessories and
weapon appearance. Unlike Ancient Cousins, where the power comes from within the biology, Bokai can visibly affect
what the character is WEARING — a leather shoulder piece develops spiritual patterns; feathers begin floating; a charm
glows; tattoo patterns spread; hair gains colored tips; a weapon gains translucent spirit extensions; animal/spirit
imagery can briefly appear behind the character.

This makes Bokai partly an OUTFIT-BASED AWAKENING.

## 13. Different awakening silhouettes

**Mandatory.** At gameplay distance, transformed races should remain identifiable WITHOUT needing to inspect aura
color.

| Race | Silhouette change |
|---|---|
| Ancient Cousin | hair + musculature + markings + energy. Flashiest traditional transformation. |
| Elf | ears + elegance + celestial traits + flowing details. Ethereal rather than explosive. |
| Beastfolk | anatomy. More animalistic. Minimal dependence on aura. |
| Dwarf | mass + material + equipment integration. Heavier and more constructed. |
| Bokai barbarian | hair + clothing + spiritual ornamentation + elemental details. The environment/spirit appears to become part of the character. |
| Human | unchanged unless altered by equipment, skills or an individual story event. |

## 14. Clothing system requirements

Clothing must NOT be permanently fused to race bodies unless anatomically necessary. Race and clothing are separate
systems.

```
BODY
 -> RACE ANATOMY
 -> UNDERLAYER
 -> TOP
 -> BOTTOM
 -> SHOES
 -> OUTERWEAR
 -> ACCESSORIES
 -> EQUIPMENT
 -> TRANSFORMATION MODIFIERS
```

- **Beastfolk** — clothing must account for tails, wings if supported, digitigrade legs where used, unusual feet,
  horns, fins and ears. Do NOT simply clip these through human clothes. Race-compatible variants should modify
  garments around anatomy.
- **Dwarf** — human clothing can inspire dwarf clothing but must be refitted to dwarven proportions. Do not simply
  scale human clothes down.
- **Elf** — clothing remains largely identical between Human Veil and Celestial Form. Only anatomy/effects requiring
  adjustment should change.
- **Ancient Cousin** — Primal transformation should preserve the player's outfit whenever possible. Clothing may react
  through loosened fit, energy illumination, small tears under extreme transformation, and transformation-specific
  accessory behavior. Do NOT automatically replace streetwear with fantasy armor.
- **Barbarian** — Bokai is allowed to modify clothing more extensively than other awakenings. However, the original
  outfit should remain visually traceable underneath the awakened version.

## 15. Character creator architecture

**RACE:** Human, Ancient Cousin, Elf, Beastfolk, Dwarf, Barbarian Culture.

**BODY:** masculine build, feminine build; build = lean / average / athletic / stocky / strong.

**FACE:** race-compatible eyes, brows, nose, mouth, jaw, facial structure, markings, ears.

**HAIR:** the existing broad hairstyle library should be preserved. Race transformations should derive alternate hair
states FROM the selected style. Do not maintain only one transformed hairstyle.

## 16. Beastfolk creator

Additional menu — **LINEAGE**: canine, feline, vulpine, lagomorph, reptilian, aquatic, avian, other.

Then **ANIMAL TRAITS**: ears, tail, scales/fur, eye type, fangs, claws, markings, crest/fins/etc.

Eventually allow enough mixing that Beastfolk do not all look identical within one lineage.

## 17. Ancient Cousin creator

Additional options: ancestral marking pattern; marking intensity; Controlled Primal hair highlight (default
blonde/gold); Primal hair evolution generated/selected based on base hairstyle.

Dark Primal automatically uses white transformed hair + red eyes + crimson ancestral markings.

## 18. Elf creator

Should eventually allow editing both the HUMAN VEIL and the CELESTIAL FORM. They should remain clearly connected — the
player should NOT effectively create two unrelated characters.

Shared: facial identity, hairstyle family, body, general identity.
Celestial-specific: ears, celestial markings, eye glow, celestial hair effects, subtle anatomical differences.

## 19. Barbarian creator

Clan/culture selection: Forest, Mountain, Desert, Frost, Storm, Ember. This primarily affects cultural clothing,
tattoo options, jewelry, starting equipment and Bokai visual expression. It should NOT determine personality.

## 20. Race and culture must remain separate

A race is biological/supernatural ancestry. A culture is where someone grew up.

An elf can grow up in the modern city. A Beastfolk can belong to an Egyptian-inspired civilization. A dwarf can grow
up around humans. An Ancient Cousin can adopt modern street culture. A human can belong to a barbarian clan.

This allows Ancient Egypt to contain MULTIPLE races. Do NOT create "the Egyptian race." Instead create Egyptian-region
humans, Egyptian-region Beastfolk, Egyptian-region dwarves, Egyptian-region elves, and so on. Their clothing and
culture connect them; their biology remains different.

## 21–23. The three clothing tests

Every playable race must pass all three.

- **Modern clothing test.** Hoodie, jeans/cargos, skate shoes, casual hairstyle. Do they still look like they belong
  in the Rolling Tides city? If not, the design is too disconnected from the game's foundation.
- **Fantasy clothing test.** Travel clothing, boots, cloak, light armor. They should now belong naturally in the
  medieval world without becoming a different character.
- **Ancient Egypt test.** Historically inspired regional clothing adapted to their anatomy. They should look like
  someone of their race who lives in or travelled through this civilization — NOT their race costume with Egyptian
  decorations pasted on top.

## 24. Transformation identity test

Remove all UI labels. Show Human, Controlled Primal, Dark Primal, Celestial Elf, Awakened Beastfolk, Stoneheart Dwarf
and Bokai Barbarian in silhouette or minimally colored renders. A player should eventually be able to distinguish
them.

If the primary difference is "this aura is blue and this aura is green," the designs have failed.

## 25. Power does not equal morality

No transformation is inherently good or evil unless later story lore explicitly establishes otherwise.

Controlled Primal ≠ good. Dark Primal ≠ evil. Celestial ≠ morally superior. Beast awakening ≠ savage personality.
Stoneheart ≠ emotionless. Bokai ≠ primitive. **Race should never dictate morality.**

## 26. Power balance

Do NOT interpret transformations as automatic superiority over races without transformations. A highly trained human
should remain dangerous. Equipment, skill, magic, experience and strategy all matter.

Transformation provides different OPTIONS rather than determining the winner before combat begins. This is especially
important for humans.

## 27. Final visual summary

| Race | Visual | Awakening |
|---|---|---|
| Human | grounded and highly customizable | none inherently; ordinary people capable of extraordinary development |
| Ancient Cousin | almost human → spectacular ancestral warrior | Controlled: original hair + gold/blonde lightning highlights + fiery orange undertone + blue aura + ancestral markings. Dark: white evolved hair + glowing red eyes + crimson/black markings + unstable dark energy |
| Elf | human disguise → celestial true form | silver/violet/moonlight/celestial; something cosmic hiding among ordinary people |
| Beastfolk | human/animal hybrid → substantially more animalistic | ancestral instinct physically resurfacing. Mammals, reptiles, lizards, aquatic/fish and other animal humanoids belong to this broad racial family |
| Dwarf | compact craftspeople → living fusion of body, earth and craftsmanship | stone/metal/amber/runes; becoming the masterwork |
| Barbarian / Bokai | human clan cultures → spiritual/elemental awakened appearance | turquoise/jade/earth/spirit foundation; body + tattoos + hair + clothing + equipment + spiritual manifestations |

## 28. The world's central race design principle

Every transformation answers a different question.

| Race | Question |
|---|---|
| Human | What can I become through my choices? |
| Ancient Cousin | What is buried in my blood? |
| Elf | What am I underneath this disguise? |
| Beastfolk | What happens when I stop suppressing my animal ancestry? |
| Dwarf | What happens when creator and creation become one? |
| Bokai | What happens when the land, my ancestors and I move as one? |

These distinctions should guide all future race design.

## 29. Implementation priority

Do NOT attempt every race simultaneously.

1. **Phase 1 — Human foundation.** Finish the body system, face system, hair system, clothing layering and movement
   styles. The current human creator establishes the technical baseline.
2. **Phase 2 — Ancient Cousin.** Reuse the human skeleton/body where possible. Implement Primal hair variants,
   ancestral markings, Controlled Primal, Dark Primal and the aura/effect system. This tests transformation
   architecture.
3. **Phase 3 — Elf.** Implement Human Veil, Celestial anatomy, ear morph, celestial markings/effects and
   transformation preservation of clothing. This tests alternate-form architecture.
4. **Phase 4 — Beastfolk.** Implement ONE lineage first (recommended: feline or canine). Build normal hybrid →
   awakened animalistic form. Once this pipeline works, expand to reptilian and aquatic anatomy.
5. **Phase 5 — Dwarf.** Build a genuinely different body proportion and refit clothing. Then prototype Stoneheart.
6. **Phase 6 — Barbarian / Bokai.** Barbarians can reuse human anatomy initially. Concentrate on clan clothing,
   tattoos, accessories, Bokai transformation modifiers and spiritual FX.

## 30. Non-negotiable rule

Do not solve race implementation by creating six completely disconnected character systems. Reuse shared systems
wherever anatomy permits. The goal is **one character framework capable of expressing many peoples.**

**Shared:** customization architecture, clothing logic, animation framework, save data, equipment, material framework,
facial-expression logic.

**Race-specific:** anatomy, morphs, markings, transformations, compatible clothing adaptations, special movement where
necessary, race-specific visual effects.

This keeps the game expandable.

## Final target

The player should eventually be able to create a curly-haired modern human skater; a loc-wearing Ancient Cousin whose
hair becomes longer with gold lightning streaks during Controlled Primal; an emo elf who looks completely human until
revealing a silver-violet Celestial Form; a mostly human-looking feline Beastfolk who becomes dramatically more feline
when awakened; a compact dwarf engineer whose equipment becomes part of their Stoneheart body; or a desert-clan human
whose clothing, tattoos and equipment erupt into turquoise-and-sand Bokai spirit imagery.

All six should look like they belong in the same game, the same world, and the same character creator.

**Different origins. Different awakenings. Same world.**
