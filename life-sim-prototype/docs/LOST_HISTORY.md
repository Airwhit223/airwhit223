# The Lost History — foundational world lore

The physical world was not always mundane. Magic did not suddenly appear, fantasy races did not come into existence
one day, and ancient supernatural civilizations were not invented by the Dream Realm. Mythology, folklore, monsters,
magic, spirits, legendary races, impossible civilizations, magical weapons, ancient heroes and supernatural places all
originated in REAL parts of the world's history. Humanity forgot because that history was deliberately removed from
ordinary reality.

## 1. The world before the Sealing
The physical world was openly connected to supernatural forces. It contained humans, dwarves, Beastfolk, elves and
celestial visitors, ancient magical cultures, spiritual traditions and peoples still undiscovered — with magical
schools, supernatural creatures, enchanted artifacts, ancient weapons, sacred sites, dimensional pathways and
civilizations interacting with other realms. The modern world inherited distorted memories of that age; they became
mythology.

## 2. The Dark Ancient Forces
At some point these forces threatened far more than individual kingdoms. Their origin stays a mystery early on — not
simply demons, aliens, gods or Dream creatures; different civilizations interpreted them differently. What matters is
what they did: destroying, corrupting and consuming the mystical foundations of the world. Peoples were threatened,
knowledge disappeared, artifacts were corrupted, civilizations fell.

## 3. The Great Sealing
Survival required an extreme solution. A massive event separated much of supernatural civilization from ordinary
reality — people, settlements, structures, artifacts, magical knowledge, historical records, creatures, pieces of
geography and pathways to other realms were pushed into what became known as the Dream Realm. The Dream Realm
therefore contains **displaced history**, not merely dreams.

## 4. Why "Dream Realm"
Dreaming became one of the few remaining ways physical consciousness could touch it — a crack in the wall. Most people
got meaningless fragments; some saw visions, real places, real people, pieces of forgotten history. Modern society
called it dreaming because it no longer had the knowledge to understand it.

## 5. The cost
Knowledge became legend, legend became folklore, folklore became fiction. Dwarves became fantasy, Beastfolk became
monsters from stories, magic became superstition, real places became legends. The physical world became convinced it
had always been ordinary. It had not.

## 6. Life inside
The Sealing did not freeze the Dream Realm. People lived, children were born, cultures evolved, alliances formed, wars
continued, settlements rose and vanished — all while surviving the same Dark Ancient Forces.

## 7. Humans and dwarves
Trapped populations became dependent on each other: humans adapted, improvised, experimented and used unfamiliar
equipment; dwarves engineered, built and made things that survived impossible conditions. Together they produced
equipment able to survive Dream Realm threats — the tradition of **Dreamforging**. Because so many allies were human,
dwarven craft became expert at human proportions, movement, fighting styles, weaknesses and adaptability. This ancient
partnership is a foundation of the modern **Gear path**.

## 8. The Keepers
Individuals responsible for preserving histories, names, maps, bloodlines, artifacts, warnings, and knowledge of both
worlds and of the Dark Ancient Forces. Not rulers — their duty was to remember what the enemy wanted forgotten.

## 9. The Keepers disappear
Killed, corrupted, lost, separated, forgotten. Records vanished, branches of knowledge ended. By the era just before
Rolling Tides, only one true Keeper remained: **Grandpa**.

## 10. Grandpa
He can first appear strange, confused, rambling, obsessed with things nobody believes and somehow tied to dreams. The
truth is larger: he carried the lost history of the world. His impossible talk was often memory — erased places,
people, warnings, events from before the modern world understood itself.

## 11. Split consciousness
His condition between physical Grandpa and Dream Grandpa came from his role as the final Keeper: part of him anchored
to physical reality, part inside the Dream Realm, each with incomplete access to the whole person. They were one
person separated by two realities.

## 12. Why he could not just explain
Not an exposition machine: his consciousness was fragmented, his memories incomplete and partly deteriorated, some
knowledge was dangerous, some could attract the Dark Ancient Forces, and some things could not be understood without
experiencing the Dream Realm. So his warnings sounded like stories, fragments, riddles and impossible claims — a
history the physical world had no context for.

## 13. The Rolling Tides hero
Grandpa's grandchild — eventually the **heir to the last Keeper**. Not an automatic magical title: they inherit
unfinished history, unanswered questions, responsibility, knowledge and consequences. Grandpa preserved the door; his
grandchild opened it.

## 14. The events of Rolling Tides
Normal life → strange events → Grandpa's mystery → the Dream Realm → supernatural powers → forgotten history → the
Dark Ancient Forces. The barrier is broken or opened, and the Sealing Era ends.

## 15. Grandpa is free
His divided existence ends and he is whole again — not everything at once, but enough. The man who sounded confused
for years can finally look at his family clearly. An emotionally important moment.

## 16. The secret is no longer his alone
The Dream Realm is open: its peoples can speak for themselves, its libraries can be explored, its ruins studied, its
descendants returned. The last Keeper can stop being the only person who remembers.

## 17. Freedom has a price
Opening the realm frees Grandpa, trapped peoples, civilizations, knowledge, magical traditions and history — and also
weakens the prison holding corrupted entities, hostile creatures, dangerous artifacts, unstable magic and the Dark
Ancient Forces. The hero frees history and what history was hiding from.

## 18. The hero's sickness
Repeated crossings and deep Dream contact leave them with **Dream Sickness**, making Dream travel especially dangerous
for them. The person who freed the Dream Realm can no longer safely live in it: Grandpa is free, his grandchild bears
part of the cost.

## 19. The Great Return
Years later, the physical world has changed enormously: trapped people have returned, descendants reunite with
physical family lines, ancient races are visible again, "fictional" cultures exist openly, artifacts are studied,
magic returns, Dreamforged technology enters modern engineering, and ordinary people find their family histories
reaching into the mythical past. This is the Great Return, part of the broader **Merging Era**.

## 20. Why there are more of every race
Returning populations, descendants born during separation, reconnected families, dormant heritage resurfacing, and
migration by previously isolated peoples. A historical consequence, not a retcon.

## 21. Grandpa in the new game
No longer the last person holding the truth — the last Keeper who lived to see the truth return. He can finally just
be Grandpa: family, stories people believe, old friends, descendants of people he knew, restored places, historians,
explorers, laughing, arguing, relaxing, grieving. Not a chair that dispenses quests.

## 22. His knowledge has limits
He preserved the history he could reach. Travel far enough and the player finds things beyond him — other planets,
Ancient Cousin history, cosmic civilizations, forces older than the Great Sealing. "I've never seen this before"
should be significant.

## 23. The cosmic reveal
The Great Sealing hid **our world's part** of mystical history. Other worlds met these forces differently: fought
them, were destroyed, sealed their own histories, never encountered them, or know exactly what they are. The
adventure expands — town → world → ancient history → Dream Realm → other worlds → cosmic history — without
invalidating Grandpa, who protected one extraordinarily important piece of a much larger puzzle.

## 24. The historical spine
Age of Myth → Ancient War → Great Sealing → Forgotten Age → the Last Keeper → Rolling Tides → the Opening → Great
Return → Merging Era → this game → Cosmic Age.

## 25. Why this game is adventure-focused
Rolling Tides was about discovery; this game begins after it, so its focus is exploration: travel, puzzles,
archaeology, ancient civilizations, Dream expeditions, other cultures, other planets, cosmic mysteries — without
abandoning the life simulation. Grandpa protected the map; the hero opened the door; the new protagonist asks where
it leads.

## 26. The player can still stay home
Grandpa's struggle was partly about giving future generations the choice earlier ones lost. The new protagonist need
not become a Keeper or a cosmic warrior, or enter the Dream Realm at all. Working, making friends, sports, music,
love, children, travelling between towns, building a home — choosing the life-sim path is not refusing the story.
Thematically it is one of the things they fought for.

## 27. The other choice
Grandpa also preserved history so someone could eventually discover it. Town → ruins → ancient Egypt → Dream Realm →
lost civilization → another continent → another planet → the Ancient Cousin homeworld → beyond. Near the end of that
journey the player can realize: Grandpa wasn't protecting the answer, he was protecting the first piece of the
question.

**Final principle:** the Dream Realm is not where fantasy came from — it is where fantasy was hidden. Grandpa did not
invent the stories; he remembered them. Rolling Tides did not create magic; it released what the world had forgotten.
This game is not about discovering that magic exists — it is about deciding what to do now that history has come home.
