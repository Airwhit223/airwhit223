# Bram — Character & Companion Bible

**Status:** Approved character direction. Implementation is pending his authored roster entry, model preset, and permanent-companion system.

## Core read

Bram is a warm, enthusiastic explorer with a huge orange travel pack, a big appetite, and a habit of treating every strange ruin like the start of a wonderful day. He appears harmless and a little clumsy at first, but he is physically formidable and relentlessly dependable when somebody needs help.

His visual source of truth is the user-provided `bram.png` reference sheet: warm brown skin, short messy black hair, thick brows, an open friendly smile, a broad soft-but-powerful build, black graphic shirt, olive cargo pants, rugged hiking boots, wrist wraps, white towel/scarf, and a worn orange expedition pack with bedroll and utility straps.

## Party role: early protector, later field expert

Bram is intentionally **very strong from the early game through the middle game**. He should make a new adventurer feel safer, rather than feel like a weak escort quest NPC.

| Player level | Bram's intended role | Balance rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1–15 | Exceptional protector | High health, strong basic attacks, crowd control, and a carry/rescue action. He can decisively handle common threats. |
| 16–30 | Reliable front-line companion | Still a leading bruiser with excellent durability and practical exploration support. He remains worth bringing to dangerous regions. |
| 31+ | Skilled but outscaled veteran | His raw damage, speed, and defenses no longer keep pace with high-level specialists and supernatural enemies. He stays useful through scouting, hauling, terrain interaction, rescue, and morale—not endgame damage. |

This is a **power-curve limitation, not a reveal that Bram was pretending to be weak**. He is genuinely strong. Later-game threats simply demand powers, techniques, or experience outside his lane.

## Mechanics to preserve the fantasy

- **Pack Mule:** increases carried quest/field items or retrieves a dropped supply without spending the player's action.
- **Steady Hands:** protects a nearby ally from a heavy hit or helps them up after a knockdown.
- **Trail Sense:** reveals climb points, old paths, campsite resources, and points of interest.
- **Big Swing:** powerful early/mid-game crowd-control attack; its damage scales modestly after level 30.
- **Field Meal:** restores a small amount of health/energy at a campsite, with a limited daily use.

Avoid giving him late-game cosmic scaling, hidden divine ancestry, or a sudden elite combat transformation. His value after level 30 is that he remains brave, useful, and emotionally steady when he is no longer the person who can solve a fight by himself.

## Story connection

Bram stays at the medieval-village inn while investigating the old routes and repair sites associated with his grandfather. His initial map-recovery quest unlocks trust, not an immediate permanent recruitment. The player should later earn the ability to invite Bram on expeditions; his expedition knowledge and field support remain useful at every level even as his combat strength falls behind.
