### What to Compare Against Codex's Work:
- **Geometry Proportions:** The house is two-story with a front porch; the general store has a 3-stripe awning and stacked crates.
- **Road Treatment:** Packed dirt transitioning to cobblestone near the shop with grass tufts breaking up edges.
- **Color Palette:** Strictly controlled 7-color palette (`#C4956A` warm wood, `#7A6B5D` roof shingle, `#D4B896` dirt, `#B8A88C` cobble, `#7DB84A` grass, metal accents). Codex should not introduce arbitrary neon or unvetted colors.

## Summary Checklist for Reviewing Codex's Assets:

| Criteria | Target (Our References) | Fail State (Reject or Fix) |
|---|---|---|
| **Render Style** | Cel-shaded, 2 tones, ink outlines | Photorealistic PBR, smooth plastic, muddy shadows |
| **Hair / Fur** | Chunky sculptural geometry masses | Realistic thin strands, flat billboard hair cards |
| **Outlines** | Inverted-hull dark ink outlines | No outlines or messy screen-space edge noise |
| **Faces** | Toriyama eyes, grounded noses, expressive | Giant chibi eyes, featureless anime doll faces |
| **Environment** | Curving, lived-in, prop-clustered | Flat endless grid, untextured greybox cubes |
| **Clothing** | Modular sockets, contemporary streetwear | Baked single-mesh costumes, medieval fantasy armor only |
